# Reimbursement receipts go to receipts@ but are never bank-matched

Toll and parking receipts uploaded with a Travel Reimbursement were paid on a staff member's own card, so no company bank or card line will ever exist for them; the cost reaches the books through payroll as Travel Reimbursement. We still send them to receipts@withers-ventures.com so every receipt lives in one archive, but the receipt processor files anything sent by Withers Time as "reimbursed via payroll" and never tries to match it to a bank line. Lyft ride reports are different: those rides are charged to the company card, so they go through receipts@ and match the card charge like any other vendor receipt.

## Considered Options

- **Keep uploads only in Withers Time, never email them.** Rejected: Alina wants one receipt archive, and the bookkeeper should see every receipt in the same place.
- **Email them and let the processor treat them like any receipt.** Rejected: the processor only knows owner, business and cash cards, so every personal-card receipt would fail and pile up for manual review, or worse, get matched to an unrelated bank line.
